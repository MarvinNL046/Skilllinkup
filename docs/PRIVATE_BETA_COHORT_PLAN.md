# Skilllinkup private-beta cohort plan

Launch mode: free, invite-only, English-first.

Local working hypothesis: Rotterdam–The Hague.

The region remains a hypothesis until the product owner records the final decision.

## Cohort segmentation

| Product | Initial demand                                    | Initial supply                               | Segment                                                                                                         |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Online  | invited clients with a real, scoped project       | verified freelancers                         | web design, WordPress, SEO, social media and copywriting; worldwide remote                                      |
| Local   | residents or small businesses with a real request | identity/contact-checked local professionals | plumbing, boiler/heating, air conditioning, electrical and carpentry in Rotterdam–The Hague                     |
| Jobs    | Dutch companies with a genuine vacancy            | candidates explicitly opting into the beta   | Netherlands-based or remote-European software, product/design, marketing, customer success and operations roles |

## Supply verification

No demand invitation is sent for a segment until at least three active supply participants pass all applicable checks:

- reachable email and phone/contact method confirmed manually;
- profile, service area, skills/trade and availability reviewed;
- portfolio, company or trade evidence reviewed without presenting it as a regulated background check;
- response expectation and beta terms acknowledged;
- Local professionals provide business identity and insurance information where applicable;
- company vacancies are confirmed on a company-controlled domain or through a named company contact;
- operator records verifier, date, evidence category and expiry/recheck date without copying unnecessary identity documents.

## Waves

### Wave 0 — internal rehearsal

- 3–5 known participants per side, using real workflows but no payments.
- Complete one committed flow in Online, Local and Jobs.
- Exercise support, report, cancellation and account-access recovery.
- Exit only after the daily log, safety escalation and rollback rehearsal have named owners.

### Wave 1 — supply-first private beta

- Online: minimum 15 active freelancers, at least 3 in each launch category; invite up to 10 project clients.
- Local: minimum 15 professionals, at least 3 in each launch trade across the working region; invite at most 10 requests and never more than current verified capacity.
- Jobs: 5 verified companies, up to 10 live vacancies and an invited candidate pool aligned to those roles.
- Invitations are issued in small batches twice per week, never automatically from a waitlist.

### Wave 2 — controlled expansion

- Requires two complete weekly reviews, no unresolved SEV-1, support ownership and sufficient sample size.
- Expand one category, city or acquisition channel at a time.
- Local expands only after existing Rotterdam–The Hague trades meet response and quality thresholds.
- Record the hypothesis, capacity, owner and rollback condition before each expansion.

## Pause and removal rules

- Pause all invitations on authentication, cross-account access, data exposure, payment activation or destructive-data incidents.
- Pause a product segment when the KPI contract’s response or trust stop condition is met.
- Remove fraudulent vacancies, unsafe Local supply or deliberately misleading profiles immediately while preserving the moderation audit trail.
- Do not compensate weak supply by buying more demand traffic.
- Resume only after the incident owner records remediation and the affected smoke path passes.

## Acquisition during the beta

Use founder-led and partner-led invitations first: professional associations and local networks for Local, curated communities and referrals for Online, and direct company outreach for Jobs. Paid acquisition remains a measured experiment after response capacity is proven; every channel gets its own invite cohort so quality and repeat use can be compared.

## Required decisions

- final Local launch boundary and travel radius;
- named cohort/product owner and invitation sender;
- participant terms and feedback consent;
- acquisition channel per wave;
- compensation, if any, for research interviews;
- explicit go/no-go timestamp in the launch checklist.

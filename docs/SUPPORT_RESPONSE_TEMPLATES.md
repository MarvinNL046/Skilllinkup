# Skilllinkup private-beta support templates

English-first templates for the free private beta. Personalise the facts and remove irrelevant text. Never request passwords, session tokens, complete identity documents or payment-card details.

## Account access

Subject: We’re helping you regain access to Skilllinkup

> Thanks for contacting Skilllinkup. We will verify the account through the email address and secure sign-in flow already connected to it. Please do not send us your password or sign-in code. If you still have a signed-in session, keep it open while we investigate. We aim to respond within one business day.

Escalate immediately on suspected takeover, changed account email or cross-account data visibility.

## No response or match yet

Subject: Update on your Skilllinkup request

> Your request is still active, but we have not yet found a qualified response that meets the private-beta criteria. We are reviewing the relevant supply manually. We will not widen your location, budget or scope without asking you first. You may cancel the request at any time from your workspace.

Do not promise a match or invent available supply.

## Free-beta payment confusion

Subject: Skilllinkup does not process payments in the private beta

> Skilllinkup’s current private beta is free and does not collect project payments, buy lead credits, hold funds in escrow or send payouts. Prices shown on listings help participants discuss scope. Do not send money because a page or message appears to request payment; report the page or message to us so we can investigate it.

Treat any active Skilllinkup payment endpoint as a SEV-1 incident.

## Cancellation or no-show

Subject: We’ve recorded your cancellation concern

> We have recorded the cancellation or no-show and will review the workspace history. Because the beta does not process payments, Skilllinkup cannot issue a payment refund. We can help document what happened, restrict unsafe or abusive accounts and support a clear closeout between participants. Please keep communication inside the Skilllinkup workspace.

Escalate Local safety concerns before ordinary service disputes.

## Urgent Local safety report

Subject: Your safety report has been prioritised

> Your report has been marked urgent. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. Stop the appointment and do not meet the other participant again while the situation is unresolved. A named safety operator will acknowledge this report within one hour while the beta is active.

Do not investigate physical danger solely through chat. Preserve the moderation record and notify the safety owner.

## Privacy export or deletion

Subject: We received your privacy request

> We received your request and will verify it against the signed-in account. An export contains only the authenticated account’s information. A deletion request remains pending while we review legal retention requirements and confirm deletion with the identity provider. We will acknowledge the next step within one business day.

Never send an export to a different email address without a reviewed identity-recovery procedure.

## Report resolved

Subject: Your Skilllinkup report was reviewed

> We reviewed your report and recorded the action in our moderation audit trail. [Plain-language resolution.] If you believe an urgent risk remains, reply through the support centre and reference the report ID. We may be unable to share private enforcement details about another account.

## Vacancy verification

Subject: We need to verify this vacancy before publishing

> Before this vacancy can remain in the private beta, please confirm it through a company-controlled email address or a named company contact. Do not send applicant identity documents to support. The vacancy stays paused until verification is complete.

## Internal escalation footer

Record: severity, affected product, internal record ID, owner, response deadline, containment, user communication and resume criteria. Never paste personal message content into a general incident channel.
